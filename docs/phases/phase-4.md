## Objective

Make the CLI schema-driven rather than compiling the palette and protocol tables into the binary. info fetches the world_info contract, prints it, and caches it under ~/.codeblox/; every batch is validated client-side against the fetched schema and palette so a bad material fails fast without a round trip.
